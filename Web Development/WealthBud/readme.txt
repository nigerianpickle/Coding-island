


Wealth Bud Vision statement:
Wealth bud is a tool that is going to bring your average joe and Actual finance gurus together in a holy unison of convenience and 
understability. Users will be able to track their budgets and add payments, as well as being able to make a payment plan. Users will also 
have the ability to easily read financial news and create mock portfolios in order to make better investment decisions. This will be the 
point for anything finance related and will prioritize usability

Users:
Students interested in finance, Finance Gurus

Usability goals:
Easy to learn (Can be measured by how long it takes for users to use all features)
Effective(Can be measured by how much user)
